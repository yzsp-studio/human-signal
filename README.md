## 🇨🇳 中文

专门治「AI 味」的写作技能：用于强制调用，或在写作/改写时出现「不要有 AI 味」「不要太假」「真情实感」「有人感」，以及收到「太像 AI」「太空泛」「太模板」「太官方」「不像我说的」这类反馈时。它只改「怎么说」，不改「说什么」——不编造、不丢信息、不弱化事实、数字、引用、来源、限定条件和风险提示。有你的风格样本时优先学习其节奏、词汇、分寸和判断方式，而不是套一套通用「人味」模板。它可以只做诊断、不做改写，也允许在原文已经自然或体裁本身需要正式工整时直接回答「不需要大改」。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 诊断文本 AI 味，指出空泛、模板化、官腔的具体位置
- 在保留事实、数字、引用与限定条件的前提下改写措辞
- 有风格样本时学习你的节奏、词汇与判断方式
- 按体裁、平台、受众、行业与公开度调整表达分寸
- 原文已自然或体裁需正式时直说「不需要大改」
- 产物优先：不靠无效碎碎念、重复或跑题伪装人味

## 🇬🇧 English

A writing skill aimed squarely at the 'AI smell' in text: invoke it on demand, or whenever a draft must stop sounding fake, vague, templated or corporate — including feedback like 'too AI', 'too generic', 'sounds nothing like me'. It changes how something is said, never what is said: no invented facts, no dropped information, and no weakening of numbers, quotes, sources, caveats or risk notes. When you supply samples of your own writing it learns your rhythm, vocabulary, judgement and sense of proportion instead of applying a generic 'human' style. It can diagnose without rewriting, and it is allowed to answer 'this needs no big rewrite' when the text is already natural or the genre demands formal polish.

### ✨ What it does

- Diagnoses AI flavour in text and points at the exact vague, templated or officialese lines
- Rewrites wording while keeping facts, numbers, quotes and caveats intact
- Learns your rhythm, vocabulary and judgement when you provide style samples
- Adjusts tone for genre, platform, audience, industry and public exposure
- Says 'no big rewrite needed' when the text is already natural or formally appropriate
- Output-first: never fakes humanity with filler, repetition or off-topic chatter
